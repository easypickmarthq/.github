# EasyPick Mart 🛒

**Fresh groceries, delivered fast — right here in Coimbatore.**

EasyPick Mart is an online grocery delivery service serving Coimbatore and surrounding areas. We bring fresh produce, daily essentials, and household items straight to your doorstep with a simple, reliable ordering experience.

---

## About

EasyPick Mart makes grocery shopping quick and hassle-free. Browse our catalog, place an order, and get it delivered — no more standing in line or fighting traffic to pick up everyday essentials.

- 📍 **Service Area:** Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu
- 🥦 **What we offer:** Fruits & vegetables, dairy, grains & staples, snacks, personal care, household supplies
- 🚚 **Delivery:** Same-day / scheduled delivery slots within our service zones
- 💳 **Payments:** Cash on delivery, UPI, cards, wallets
